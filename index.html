ArcGIS Pro Network Analyst Kriging 2SFCA Method Census Data NAD83 ￼ REMOTE SENSING & DISASTER MAPPING Melamchi Flood Change Detection — Nepal Performed multitemporal change detection using Landsat 8 multispectral imagery (8 bands, 30m resolution) to map the environmental impact of the 2021 Melamchi River flood disaster in Nepal. Acquired and stacked pre-flood (February 2021) and post-flood (March 2022) scenes in ERDAS Imagine, computed NDVI for both dates, and applied image differencing to isolate changed pixels. The resulting raster classifies change into three categories: red (negative change — vegetation loss, flood scour, and sediment deposition), blue (no significant change), and green (positive change — vegetation recovery). The spatial extent and magnitude of damage visible in the red zones reflects the destructive path of floodwaters through the Melamchi Valley.
