Healthcare Accessibility Modeling — Hays County, TX

Analyzed spatial accessibility to emergency healthcare facilities across Hays County — one of the fastest-growing counties in Texas. Remote Sensing & Disaster Mapping

Melamchi Flood Change Detection — Nepal

Performed multitemporal change detection using Landsat 8 multispectral imagery (8 bands, 30m resolution) to map the environmental impact of the 2021 Melamchi River flood disaster in Nepal. Acquired and stacked pre-flood (February 2021) and post-flood (March 2022) scenes in ERDAS Imagine, computed NDVI for both dates, and applied image differencing to isolate changed pixels. The resulting raster classifies change into three categories: red (negative change — vegetation loss, flood scour, and sediment deposition), blue (no significant change), and green (positive change — vegetation recovery). The spatial extent and magnitude of damage visible in the red zones reflects the destructive path of floodwaters through the Melamchi Valley.

LiDAR & Urban Change Detection

Gentrification Patterns via LiDAR — Austin, TX

Processed LiDAR point cloud data for San Marcos / Austin region using LP360 to analyze spatial-temporal patterns of urban land cover change linked to gentrification. Generated and compared Canopy Height Models (CHM), Digital Surface Models (DSM), and Digital Terrain Models (DTM) between 2008 and 2021 to identify shifts in vegetation cover and built environment density.

Network Analysis & Risk Modeling

Least-Cost Path — Nuclear Waste Transport via Railroad

Developed a least-cost path analysis using U.S. railroad network data and nuclear facility locations to optimize hazardous waste transport routes from Pantego, TX to Concord, MA. The model minimized population exposure risk by integrating county-level population density (Census 2000) as a cost surface, identifying routes that avoid high-density areas while maintaining railroad feasibility.

Network Coverage Optimization — Mobile Tower Placement

Applied viewshed analysis in ArcGIS Pro to model and optimize the coverage area of mobile network towers across a mountainous terrain. Used sun azimuth calculations and DEM-based visibility modeling to identify tower placement that maximizes population coverage.
